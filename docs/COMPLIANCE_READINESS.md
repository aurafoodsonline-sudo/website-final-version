# Legal, Tax, and Labeling Readiness

Application-side readiness:

- Supplier has tax identifier, tax category, and withholding-rate placeholders.
- Supplier invoices have tax-inclusive flag, tax amount, and withholding amount placeholders.
- Finished SKUs support grammage, barcode, and label version.
- Finished batches carry batch number, packing date, expiry/best-before date, and quantity.
- Supplier payable and supplier advance remain separately auditable through ledger rows.
- Informational credit notes create adjustment documents without ledger mutation.

Food label readiness fields:

- Product/SKU code and name.
- Net weight or grammage.
- Batch number.
- Packing date.
- Expiry/best-before date.
- Label version.
- Barcode.

Required external approvals:

- Local food labeling counsel review.
- Tax consultant review of tax rates, withholding treatment, invoice format, and statutory exports.
- UAT sign-off by procurement, warehouse, production, packing, accounts, and owner/admin users.

This document is not legal or tax approval. It only records application-side readiness.
